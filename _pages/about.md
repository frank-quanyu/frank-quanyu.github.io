I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Materials Science and Engineering** at **Zhejiang University**, China.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of **condensed matter physics, computational chemistry of energy landscapes, and materials science**.

In the summer of 2026, I conducted research in **Professor David J. Wales's group at the University of Cambridge**, exploring potential energy landscapes and structural reconstruction of point defect in materials.

I am particularly interested in understanding how microscopic interactions and atomic-scale mechanism give rise to emergent physical properties.
